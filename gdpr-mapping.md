# GDPR & UK DPA 2018 Mapping

| Issue | GDPR Article | DPA 2018 Principle | Compliance Gap |
|-------|--------------|--------------------|----------------|
| Insecure HTTP transport | Art. 5(1)(f), Art. 32 | Security | No encryption for personal data in transit |
| Missing security headers | Art. 25, Art. 32 | Privacy-by-design | Insufficient hardening measures |
| Excessive logging of PII | Art. 5(1)(c), Art. 30 | Data minimisation | Logs contain unnecessary personal data |
| Weak password policy | Art. 32 | Security | Inadequate protection of user accounts |
